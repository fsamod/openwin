# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-12

- Light and dark themes. Pull the header cord to switch instantly; phones get a small theme button.
- New Why, Privacy, Security, and Contact pages, plus a 404 page.
- New icons, favicons, web app manifest, and social image.
- Search now covers descriptions and categories, with a category filter.
- Beta labels for preview apps.
- Fixed dead links, publishers, categories, and descriptions in the catalog.
- The cord settles straight and no longer shows a focus box.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-21

- First stable release with 231 apps in 11 categories.
- Exact winget commands, shareable selections, and English/German UI.
