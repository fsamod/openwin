Privacy

Privacy

OpenWin is a static site. No accounts, cookies, analytics, ads, or tracking. Here is the rest.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

On your device

OpenWin keeps three small values in localStorage:

  • openwin-selection-v1 keeps the apps you picked. It clears when you clear the selection.
  • openwin-lang-v1 keeps your language choice, if you change it.
  • openwin-theme-v1 keeps your light or dark choice, if you pull the cord. Otherwise OpenWin follows your device theme.

They stay in your browser until you clear site data.

In the URL

Selected apps appear in the fragment, like #apps=Brave.Brave,7zip.7zip. It contains package IDs, not personal information, so you can share the link. Browsers do not send the fragment to the server.

Hosting and email

GitHub Pages serves the site and may keep standard request logs. If you email fsamod@proton.me, Proton Mail processes your address and message. I only use them to reply.
